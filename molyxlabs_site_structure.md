# Molyx Labs Site Structure

## Goal

Turn the site from a generic automation studio into a focused conversion path for UK letting agencies arriving from cold outreach, LinkedIn, referrals, and early search traffic.

## Recommended Site Map

1. Home
2. Letting Agencies
3. Workflows
4. About
5. Contact
6. Case Studies

## Page-by-Page Structure

### 1. Home

Purpose:
Give cold traffic an immediate understanding of who you help, what you automate, and what the next step is.

Sections:
- Hero with niche-specific headline and `Book a Free Automation Audit` CTA
- Pain section focused on admin-heavy letting agency workflows
- Workflow examples
- Why Molyx / founder credibility
- Simple process
- ROI / time-saving framing
- Final CTA

Primary conversion:
Audit booking or contact form submission

### 2. Letting Agencies

Purpose:
This should be the main sales page for your first niche. Every cold email and LinkedIn message to letting agencies should point here, not the generic homepage.

Suggested URL:
`/letting-agency-automation`

Sections:
- Hero: `Automation for UK letting agencies`
- Specific pain points:
  maintenance coordination
  contractor chasing
  landlord updates
  tenant onboarding
  compliance reminders
  invoice/admin bottlenecks
- Workflow examples with before/after
- Why these workflows matter:
  hours saved
  fewer missed steps
  faster response times
  less key-person dependency
- Founder credibility
- FAQ
- CTA to book audit

Primary conversion:
Book an audit

### 3. Workflows

Purpose:
Show the actual types of systems you build without forcing people to infer your offer.

Suggested structure:
- Maintenance workflow automation
- Tenant onboarding automation
- Lead response automation
- Reporting automation
- Invoice and chasing automation
- Data sync and approvals

For each workflow include:
- The problem
- What gets automated
- Tools that can connect
- Expected operational benefit

Primary conversion:
CTA after each workflow: `Want this mapped to your business? Book an audit.`

### 4. About

Purpose:
Build trust. This page should answer: why should I trust you to touch operational systems in my business?

Sections:
- What Molyx does
- Founder background
- How you approach implementation
- Who you work with
- Why small businesses choose a founder-led specialist
- CTA

Primary conversion:
Contact or audit booking

### 5. Contact

Purpose:
Reduce friction and clarify what happens next.

Sections:
- Headline: `Book a free automation audit`
- Short explanation of what the audit covers
- Response time expectation
- Form
- Direct email and phone

Primary conversion:
Form completion

### 6. Case Studies

Purpose:
Give proof. Even if you only have one pilot initially, this page matters.

Early version:
- Use one detailed pilot case study
- Add one or two `example workflow breakdown` pieces while real case studies are still being built

For each case study:
- Client type
- Original problem
- Workflow built
- Time saved
- Business outcome
- Stack used

Primary conversion:
Book an audit

## Navigation Recommendation

Top nav:
- Home
- Letting Agencies
- Workflows
- About
- Contact

Button in nav:
- Book Audit

Remove:
- Generic portfolio-style labels like `Work` unless you are showing actual business results
- Anything ecommerce-related such as the cart icon if you are not selling products

## Homepage Messaging Rules

- Say who the site is for in the first screen
- Name the workflows you solve
- Avoid generic lines like `simple automations` without context
- Avoid agency filler like `comfortable working internationally`
- Avoid pretending there is a bigger team if there is not

## Conversion Improvements

- Fix the broken homepage CTA immediately
- Add a scheduling link if possible
- Show founder photo and short bio
- Add at least one concrete example with time saved
- Add a clear response-time promise on contact page
- Add a proper meta description to each page
- Replace default Squarespace social profiles with real ones or remove them
