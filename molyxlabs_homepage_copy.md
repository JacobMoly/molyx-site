## Problem Section

Headline:
Your team should not be spending hours every week chasing the same tasks

Body:
Most letting agencies already have the hard part in place: properties, tenants, landlords, contractors, and the systems that keep the business moving.

What slows everything down is the admin between those moving parts.

Maintenance issues get passed around manually. Contractors need chasing. Landlords want updates. Compliance dates have to be remembered. Invoices need logging. Information lives across inboxes, spreadsheets, and property systems.

That is exactly the kind of work we automate.
